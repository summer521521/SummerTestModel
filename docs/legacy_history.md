# Historical benchmark reference

SummerTestModel Benchmark 1.0-rc1 is the normalized starting point for current and future model comparisons.

Earlier V1, V2, and incremental experiments remain under [`benchmark_20260629/`](../benchmark_20260629/) and [`legacy_evidence/`](../legacy_evidence/). They use different tasks, runners, coverage, and scorer semantics. They are retained for historical audit only and must not be merged into current RC1 results or rankings.
